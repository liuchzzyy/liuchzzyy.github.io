I am a Ph.D. candidate in Materials Science at the Institute of Materials Science of Barcelona (ICMAB-CSIC) and the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona. My research focuses on aqueous Zn–MnO<sub>2</sub> batteries and operando synchrotron characterization techniques.

My doctoral work investigates the reaction mechanisms and capacity limitations of MnO<sub>2</sub> in rechargeable zinc chemistry using operando synchrotron techniques. Previously, I developed liquid-alloy interlayers to suppress dendrite growth on zinc and sodium metal anodes.

I also use spectral decomposition and reproducible Python workflows for data processing, materials-analysis evidence organization, code and figure generation, and exploratory writing.
